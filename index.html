Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Overview

The Fury Force game (developer: Akkelte) respects your privacy. We do not collect or store personal user data on our own servers.

Third-party services

The game uses the following third-party services, which may collect data in accordance with their own policies:

  • Google AdMob — to serve ads, which may be personalized. Google Ads policy
  • Google Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage statistics to help us improve the game. Firebase privacy

Data collected by third parties

These services may collect app activity (e.g. sessions and in-game events), device identifiers such as the advertising ID, approximate location and other diagnostic data. Data is encrypted in transit.

Data deletion

You can request deletion of data associated with your device by emailing akkelte.games@gmail.com. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Age rating

The game is rated PEGI 7 (mild violence). It is not directed primarily at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
